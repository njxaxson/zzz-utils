# Support

**Job:** buffs and utility. Personal damage is negligible and is not what the slot is for.

A support is judged almost entirely on *what the rest of the team can use*. That is the one
thing worth internalising about the class: a support's raw buff list means nothing on its own,
and the same unit is excellent behind one carry and close to worthless behind another.

Astra is the standard example. Her crit-damage buff is enormous behind an attacker and worth
essentially nothing behind an [armorer](armorer.md), whose crit damage is fixed.


Units from other classes can behave as supports, even though they are natively a different class. Most defense agents act as supports, and some damage-class agents like Orphie and Remielle can be pseudosupports. 

## Fit, not size

The engine prices this through cohesion: a support that brings a lot the team cannot use scores
*worse* than a smaller support whose whole kit lands. That is deliberate, and it is why the
strongest supports are not simply the ones with the biggest numbers.

Read [cohesion](../engine/cohesion.md) before changing anything here — the mechanism has been
rebuilt once and the wrong designs are recorded.

## Wheelchairs

Some support *pairings* uplift almost any compatible carry — Astra + Nicole for attack,
Nangong + Yuzuha or Nangong + Sunna for anomaly, Dialyn + Lucia for rupture. These are
[wheelchairs](wheelchairs.md), and they are emergent: nobody told the engine those pairs are
good, they score well because of the buffs the two units supply.

## Evolution

* 1.0
  * Four supports at launch, none of them limited: standard S-rank Rina, and A-ranks Nicole (handed out as an initial unit to the player), Soukaku and Lucy. Each is tied to an early premium team — **Lycaon/Ellen/Rina**,
    **Lycaon/Ellen/Soukaku**, **Koleda/Soldier 11/Lucy**, and less so Anby/Nekomata/Nicole. 
  * Most players prefer dexterous, easy-to-play Lucy over the slow Soukaku.  
* 1.3
  * Lighter is released, and Lighter/Lucy becomes an early wheelchair for those that pull Lighter. 
* 1.4
  * Soukaku anchors the game's strongest team, **Lycaon/Miyabi/Soukaku**, and the far more
    playable **Miyabi/Yanagi/Soukaku**.
  * For those that pulled Lighter, Lighter/Miyabi/Lucy is another strong contender. 
* 1.5
  * **Astra is the first limited S-rank support**, and the class changes character. Players
    discover that running Astra *and* Nicole behind a carry is stronger than a conventional
    third slot, which establishes the double-support wheelchair — most often
    Miyabi/Astra/Nicole rather than any attack line.
  * Lucy’s value craters because Astra does literally everything at least twice as better. 
* 2.1
  * Yuzuha, the second limited S-rank support, immediately supplants Astra on anomaly teams and
    holds that position for roughly a year. **Miyabi/Vivian/Yuzuha** dominates for a long stretch.
* 2.3
  * Lucia releases and becomes the premier rupture support, a position she still holds.
* 2.6
  * Sunna releases with Angels of Delusion. She is primarily an attack support but switches to
    anomaly support alongside another AoD member, and she is best-in-slot for Ye Shunguong's
    ether-veil gimmick. 
  * Later playtesting shows she is generally better than Astra for attack teams outright, on higher personal damage and daze output.
* 3.1
  * Although not a native support unit, Remielle takes over Yuzuha's slot in premium anomaly teamn. See [triple anomaly](triple-anomaly.md).
