# Wheelchairs

A "wheelchair" is a support pairing that uplifts almost any compatible carry — you can slot
nearly anyone into the third seat and get a good team. This is because starting with a base 
pair of strong units (stun + support, double support, subdps + support, etc.), especially
a pair of T0 units, naturally leads to a strong foundation.

This list is not exhaustive; powerful stunners and supports will naturally create a framework for 
interchangeable DPS agents to slot in as the third member to match enemy weakness. 

## Why this list matters

These pairings are **emergent**. The engine is not told that any of them are good; they score
highly because of the buffs and utility those units supply, evaluated one mechanic at a time.

So a high wheelchair score is evidence the mechanics are modelled well. If one of these
pairings stopped topping its ladder after a change, that is a signal something in the
mechanics broke — not a signal that a template needs updating.

The exception worth knowing about is the Remielle track, where the engine could not initially see
Velina's contribution and two relationships had to be declared.  Much of that has since been fixed. 
That story is on the [triple-anomaly](triple-anomaly.md) page.
