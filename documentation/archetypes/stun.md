# Stun

**Job:** create the damage window. Stunners are not pure enablers — they deal real damage too.

Most carry damage in this game lands inside a stun window, so for the three window-dependent
carry classes the stunner is not a tax, it is what makes the carry work at all. On a stun-shill
boss the window *is* the damage gate, and a team without a stunner is failing a hard requirement
rather than merely scoring lower.

Two archetypes are built by changing this slot's count rather than its occupant:
[totalize](totalize.md) runs **two** stunners, and [stunless](stunless.md) runs **none**.

## They deal real damage, and that changes team-building

Treating a stunner as a pure enabler is the most common way to misread a ladder. Several
stunners carry a second job:

| Stunner | The second job |
|----|----|
| Nangong | A full ether anomaly agent in her own right, not a stunner who buffs anomaly |
| Norma | A genuine sub-DPS; she also converts sheer buffs into personal ATK |
| Roxy | Supplies a Laceration buff, which points her at [armorers](armorer.md) |
| Koleda | Supplies Laceration at P6, likewise |

This matters for element resistance. A stunner whose element is resisted is *penalised, not disqualified*, because its damage is not the point — but the more of a unit's value sits in its damage, the worse that approximation fits. See [element resistance and role](../engine/element-resistance.md).

## Evolution

* 1.0
  * Three stunners at launch: A-rank Anby (the first unit a player receives), and standard S-ranks Koleda and Lycaon.
  * Lycaon anchors the premium ice teams (**Lycaon/Ellen/Rina**, **Lycaon/Ellen/Soukaku**); Koleda anchors
    **Koleda/Soldier 11/Lucy**.
* 1.1
  * Qingyi releases and supplants Anby, making **Qingyi/Zhu Yuan/Nicole**. She goes on to anchor
    the early anomaly hypercarry teams too (Qingyi/Yanagi/Seth).
* 1.3
  * Lighter releases and becomes best-in-slot for Ellen and Soldier 11.
* 1.6
  * Trigger and A-rank Pulchra are released along with SAnby to form aftershock team **Trigger/SAnby/Pulchra**,
  * While aftershock-centric teams peter out, Trigger remains a solid stunner. Her defense shred later turns out to matter far more to armorers than to the aftershock teams he was built for.
* 2.0
  * Ju Fufu is introduced with the rupture class, designed as best-in-class for Yixuan. It does
    not last.
* 2.4
  * **Dialyn outclasses every stunner in the game** and becomes best-in-slot for every attack and rupture team at once, supplanting Ju Fufu everywhere except fire-weak bosses. Her free ultimates will also make her the one stunner worth bringing to future stunless teams.
* 2.6
  * Lycaon's potential vision unlock lets him stun for anomaly agents other than Miyabi, and
    incidentally improves him on attack and rupture teams.
* 2.7
  * Nangong releases as a full-blown anomaly stunner — the only stunner with the same game impact
    as Dialyn. She performs every function of an ether anomaly agent *on top of* stunning and
    buffing anomaly, which rewrites anomaly team-building and drops Vivian two full tiers
    overnight.
* 3.0
  * Norma releases, uniquely compatible with Lucia and Pan, and becomes the alternative to the
    heavily contested Dialyn on rupture teams.
* 3.2
  * Roxy releases as an armorer-centric stunner who also works well for attack agents that benefit from wind anomaly procs.
  * Koleda is retrofitted to join with armorers and receives a potential vision unlock with buffs that increase her wider relevance.
