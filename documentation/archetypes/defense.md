# Defense

**Job:** shields, healing and mitigation — almost always with buffs attached.

**Despite the name, defense units are not played defensively.** The game rewards aggression, so
in practice a defender is an alternate [support](support.md) that happens to hit slightly harder.
Ben is the clearest case: he converts DEF into crit damage, and the people who run him build him
as a carry.

This is a design decision the game made *after* the class already existed. Ben is the one
original defense unit who predates it, which is why he reads oddly next to the others.

The practical consequence for team-building is that the support slot in most archetypes is
interchangeable with a defender. [Attack](attack.md) teams in particular are written as
"stunner + attacker + support **or** defense" for exactly this reason.

## Why they are penalised rather than disqualified

A DPS whose element a boss resists is disqualified outright and the team is not scored. A
defender is only penalised, because its damage is not the point. Same rule as supports — see
[element resistance and role](../engine/element-resistance.md).

## Evolution

* 1.0
  * Ben launches as the original defense unit, before the "defenders are alternate supports"
    decision that shapes everyone after him.
* 1.1
  * A-rank Seth releases alongside Jane Doe and becomes her best-in-slot partner, anchoring both
    Qingyi/Jane/Seth and the assault-based Piper/Jane/Seth. He goes on to support the early
    Yanagi teams too.
* 1.2
  * Caesar is released. She has an attack buff and provides shields and interrupt resistance; and has heavy daze. She is clearly intended as a pseudostunner.  She will quickly languish without a meaningful home; patch 2.4 will bring Banyue who enjoys her interrupt resistance but Caesar doesn’t do enough to earn a real slot on his teams.
* 2.0
  * A-rank Pan Yinhu arrives with the rupture class as its initial support, and remains one of
    the two units the [rupture](rupture.md) shape is written around.
* 2.5
  * Zhao is given away free alongside Ye Shunguong, and is one of the two units that can satisfy
    her ether-veil requirement. **YSG/Zhao/Sunna** becomes a premier attack team — an unusually
    high-profile home for a defense unit.
* 3.1
  * Caesar is added to the 50/50 loss pool, cementing her as no longer likely to receive any buffs.
