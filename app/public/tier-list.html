Recent Changes

  • 3.3, first banner: Phoenix debuts at T0.5. Phoenix appears to have the highest damage ceiling of any unit so far, even more than the void hunters and grandmasters - but only when she is on a team with Velina and Remielle. Without them, her overall potential falls dramatically, and that kind of limitation in team construction prevents her from achieving a T0 rank.
  • 3.2: Claret and Roxy debut at T0.5. Claret is a very capable electric DPS and has great low-cost team options. Time will tell if she can ascend to be a dominant carry like the other T0 DPS options, but for now, she sits comfortably in T0.5. Roxy enters as a good stunner and subdps for all crit-DPS teams: she has no unique or dominating features, but does her job well and serves a wide array of team compositions.
